Where are you on the founder journey?

Ideas can begin inside an existing business, as a side hustle outside of work, or a personal project you've always wanted to give a solid go. The path from frustration (or dream) to a product people can rely on typically hits a roadblock or two along the way. Which of these stages do you connect with the most?

Still alongside your work

Finding the opportunity

You notice a better way while working a job or running an existing business.

The frustration

Slow processes, repetitive work, or software that is too complicated or simply does not do what you need.

Your idea takes shape

You can see a product that would save time, improve the work, or solve a problem other people have too.

Building around the day job

There is a lot to work out but you're struggling for time. You've started defining the product, making a prototype or automation in excel, or a practical first version.

From idea to prototype

You know what you want to create, but not always where to start, who to talk to, who to trust. A vibe-coded or offshored prototype may need direction, rebuilding, refinement, or stronger security.

Customers and funding

People are using it and paying, that's fantastic! Your clients are starting to request features, or your product is showing signs of struggling under the load. This is often where an idea becomes a full-time commitment.

Scaling with confidence

The product, team, and technical foundations are growing, and it feels like your business is being pulled away from your vision. Maintaining the clarity that made the idea valuable is crucial.
